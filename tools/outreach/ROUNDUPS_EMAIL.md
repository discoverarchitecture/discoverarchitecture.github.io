## The email

**Subject:** A free site for your [list name] list

Hi [Author's first name],

I read your [list name] and thought you might like to know about [Find Great Architecture](link), a free site I built
for architecture students.

I trained as an architect at CEPT University and found that finding good precedents meant a lot of searching. So the
site works the other way round: students browse a wall of buildings, by style, type, city or architect, and open any
one for:

- photos that are free to reuse in coursework, each credited with its licence and source;
- the architect, year, place and key features, with links to plans and drawings where they exist;
- related buildings, books and architects to follow next.

There are no ads, no sign-up and no paywall. [One line that fits the list, from the table, e.g. "Since your list is
about free images, the credited photos may be the most useful part."]

If it fits, I'd be glad to see it added. Either way, thanks for putting the list together; I found [one real site from
their list] through it.

Best wishes,
Akshat
findgreatarchitecture.com

## Rules for this email

- Only say what is true on the day it is sent (check: no ads, no sign-up, free photos with credits).
- The last line names a site from their own list that you really looked at; if none, drop that clause.
- No offer of a link back, payment or a guest post: that turns a recommendation into a deal.
- Never mention exact totals of buildings or books (CLAUDE.md).
