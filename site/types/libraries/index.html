Henri Labrouste's Bibliothèque Sainte-Geneviève in Paris, opened in 1851, leaves its slender cast-iron columns and arches visible over the reading room; it is called a major step towards modern architecture, and it influenced the Boston Public Library, McKim Building (1895). Gordon Bunshaft's Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library in New Haven (1963) filters light through translucent marble panels onto a glass-walled book tower. Toyo Ito's Sendai Mediatheque (2001) carries thin floors on thirteen lattice tubes. OMA's Seattle Central Library (2004) stacks shifted platforms inside a diamond-mesh skin, and its Books Spiral keeps the nonfiction collection in one continuous run.

Questions students ask

What makes good library architecture?

Good library architecture both stores books and draws people to them, and across history the weight has shifted from archive towards public room. Libraries now also serve as community hubs. Helsinki Central Library Oodi (2018) spans an open ground floor with a timber wave clad in spruce, beneath a top-floor reading room known as book heaven, and Dokk1 in Aarhus (2015) brings library and citizens' services together on the waterfront.

Which libraries should architecture students study?

Start with the Bibliothèque Sainte-Geneviève by Henri Labrouste, opened in 1851 in Paris and seen as a major step on the way to modern architecture for its exposed cast-iron columns and arches. Then compare Gunnar Asplund's Stockholm Public Library (1928), Alvar Aalto's Viipuri Library (1935), lit by round conical skylights, the Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library (1963), the Geisel Library in San Diego (1970) and Mecanoo's Library of Birmingham (2013).

How has library design changed over time?

Over time the emphasis has shifted from keeping records to welcoming the public. The earliest libraries, such as Mesopotamia's Library of Ashurbanipal, were archives of early writing. Growing collections later ran out of room, which brought in compact shelving that moves on tracks. The United States built 1,689 Carnegie libraries from 1883 to 1929, and modernism shaped the public libraries of the 1960s. Recent libraries add public rooms, from Oodi in Helsinki to Calgary Central Library (2018).

What are good examples of modern libraries?

Alberto Kalach's Vasconcelos Library in Mexico City (2006) hangs steel book stacks in a tall glazed hall within a botanical garden. Snøhetta's Bibliotheca Alexandrina (2002) lights a terraced reading room through a tilted disc roof, behind a granite wall of scripts. Mecanoo's Library of Birmingham (2013) stacks boxes wrapped in interlocking metal rings, with a rotunda reading room and garden terraces, and Oodi in Helsinki (2018) opens its ground floor to the city.
