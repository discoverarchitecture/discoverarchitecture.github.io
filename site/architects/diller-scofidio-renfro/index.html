Diller Scofidio + Renfro

Founded 1981 · American · 4 buildings on the wall

Diller Scofidio + Renfro is a New York design studio that works across architecture, the visual arts and the performing arts. It was founded in 1981 by the architects Elizabeth Diller and Ricardo Scofidio; Charles Renfro joined in 1997 and became a partner in 2004, and Benjamin Gilmartin became the fourth partner in 2015. In 1999 it was the first architecture firm to receive a MacArthur Fellowship, the foundation saying it had "created an alternative form of architectural practice that unites design, performance, and electronic media with cultural and architectural theory and criticism".

Read moreShow less

The Institute of Contemporary Art, Boston (2006) was one of the studio's first buildings and the first new art museum in Boston in over a century. The High Line in New York (2009), designed with James Corner Field Operations and Piet Oudolf, turned a disused 1.45-mile railway spur on Manhattan's West Side into an elevated park that opened in phases from 2009 to 2014. Zaryadye Park in Moscow (2017), beside Red Square, came from a 2012 competition its team won against 90 entries, with terraces planted as forest, steppe, tundra and floodplain.
