Not jury-ready? A free redo or your money back

Not jury-ready? Within 7 days of getting your files, email hello@findgreatarchitecture.com with your order number. You can choose one free redo, or a full refund to your original payment method. Refunds go through Dodo Payments and usually arrive within 5–10 business days. One refund per project.

How the free redo works

  1. Open the link in your receipt email.
  2. Change your answers or your drawings, if you like.
  3. Choose Redo. There's no charge.

If something goes wrong on our side

If a paid build fails or never arrives, email us with your receipt and we will run it again at no cost.

Payments

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. If you were charged twice or see a charge you don't recognise, email us first and we'll sort it out with Dodo.
