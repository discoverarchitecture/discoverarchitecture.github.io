## The copy

- **Name:** Studio FGA
- **Website:** the tracking link from the table above
- **Tagline (60 max):** Messy architecture drawings to a jury-ready A1 board
- **Short description (160 max):** Upload your plans, sections and sketches. Get a jury-ready A1 architecture board and a
  10–15 slide deck, editable. Free preview; from $11.
- **Long description:**
  Studio FGA is an AI architecture presentation generator for students and studios. Upload what you have (plans,
  sections, sketches, model photos, even screenshots of your sheets), answer a few quick questions, and see a free
  preview of a jury-ready A1 board and a matching 10–15 slide jury deck: which drawing goes where and what each slide
  says. Move, swap or remove anything. Pay once to get the files: your drawings cleaned up and lined up, new images
  (up to 10 with Polish, up to 15 with Transform, which also redraws the hero view, plans and sections), and captions,
  labels, scale bars and north. Download PDF, PowerPoint and InDesign, and keep editing in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google
  Slides, Canva or InDesign. Made by Find Great Architecture, a free site for architecture students, by an architect
  trained at CEPT University.
- **Pricing:** Free preview (two a day). Then one payment per project: Polish US$11 or Transform US$16. No
  subscription. One free redo within 7 days; no refunds.
- **Pricing model (for forms):** Freemium / Paid, one-time payment
- **Categories / tags:** Design, Architecture, Education, Students, Presentations, Productivity
- **Who it's for:** architecture students preparing a jury, crit or final review; small studios preparing competition
  boards
- **Images (in this folder, 2540 × 1520):** `launch/1-hero.jpg`, `launch/2-how-it-works.jpg`,
  `launch/3-before-after.jpg`, `launch/4-what-you-get.jpg`, `launch/5-pricing.jpg`. Logo: the live
