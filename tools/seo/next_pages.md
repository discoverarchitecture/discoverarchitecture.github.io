SEO chat, 5 Nov 2026, from the owner's request: "start with the best architecture books for students page … keep nudging
me to do the next part … whenever you're starting a new batch, ask for permission and go on."

Why so few, so slowly: Google cut most of the site on 25 Oct 2026 (CLAUDE.md, "Which building pages Google may index").
Thousands of quick auto-made pages are what caused it, so each new page here is hand-chosen, checked by eye, and better
than what ranks today. Bing, ChatGPT search and Copilot pick each page up within minutes (IndexNow); Google is expected
to take 6–8 weeks to re-judge the site.

How each page is made (one batch = one page, or a small set of closely related ones):
1. Ask the owner before starting the batch.
2. Haiku: gather candidates from the site's own data (books seed, films seed, buildings) and re-check links.
3. Sonnet: draft the page file from the brief, using only facts from the data or a cited source; a second Sonnet run
   checks every fact.
4. Opus: choose and order, check every cover or photo by eye, build, check at 390 px and desktop, day and night.
5. Pull request; the owner says "merge"; tick it off here.
