Abdel-Wahed El-Wakil

Egypt · 1943 · 4 projects · 0 plans & drawings

Abdel-Wahed El-Wakil (born 1943) is an architect from Egypt. Roark holds 4 buildings by Abdel-Wahed El-Wakil, dated 1987 to 2012, in 2 countries (3 in Saudi Arabia and 1 in Brunei). Among them: 4 religious buildings. No plans or drawings of this work are in the archive yet.

Abdel-Wahed El-Wakil is an Egyptian architect who has designed over fifteen mosques in Saudi Arabia and is considered by many as the foremost contemporary authority in Islamic architecture.
