About this wall

Find Great Architecture is a huge, growing wall of great buildings, curated for architects, architecture students and anyone who loves buildings: the modern masters, contemporary and parametric work, brutalism and regionalism. Wander it instead of searching. Every building has free photos with credits, its key features, and links to plans, sections and articles for a case study. Spin the globe to explore buildings from all over the world, see them all A–Z, or test yourself with the guess-the-building games. Short building videos on YouTube.
