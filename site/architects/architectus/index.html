Architectus

Australian and New Zealand · 4 buildings on the wall

Architectus is an architecture and design practice with 13 studios across Australia and New Zealand, from Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane to Auckland and Christchurch. With more than 800 architects, interior designers, landscape architects, urban planners and other specialists, it describes itself as the largest practice in its region. Its work covers commercial, defence, infrastructure, public and transport projects, with education and health as its two largest areas, and many of its buildings were designed together with partners from abroad.

Read moreShow less

Early landmarks include the Gallery of Modern Art in Brisbane (2006). 1 Bligh Street in Sydney (2011), an office tower designed with the German firm Ingenhoven Architects, has a naturally ventilated atrium running its full 120-metre height and won the International High Rise Award in 2012. The Queen Elizabeth II Courts of Law in Brisbane (2012) won the Australian Institute of Architects' national award for public architecture in 2013. With SANAA the practice worked on the Art Gallery of New South Wales, North Building (Sydney Modern, Naala Nura) (2022), which won a national award for public architecture in 2023.
