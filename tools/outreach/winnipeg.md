# Winnipeg buildings (from a librarian's suggestion)

Liv Valmestad, Art Librarian at the University of Manitoba (Architecture/Fine Arts Library) and past president of
ARLIS/NA, agreed on 6 Nov 2026 to add the site to her guide, and pointed out that the wall has only two Winnipeg
buildings (Manitoba Hydro Place and Saint Boniface Cathedral). She suggested the Winnipeg Building Index, made by the
University of Manitoba Libraries, as the source.

For the data collection chat: add these through the usual pipeline (free Commons photos only, the main photo shows the
building, checked by eye). Add the Winnipeg Building Index entry to each one's Go deeper links where one exists.
