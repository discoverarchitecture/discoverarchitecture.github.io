Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers purchases of Studio FGA boards and decks from findgreatarchitecture.com. It is the same policy as Studio FGA’s own Refund policy. Studio FGA is run by Akshat Pagaria, 302, Shantinath Residency, Beawar, Rajasthan, India. The rest of the website is free and has nothing to refund.

No refunds

Each board and deck is made for you as soon as you pay, using paid AI processing. So all sales are final and we don’t offer refunds.

You see a free preview of your board and deck, and the price (Polish US$11 or Transform US$16, see Pricing), before you pay, so you know what you’re getting.

One free redo

If the result isn’t right, you get one free redo of your board and deck within 7 days of paying:

  1. Open the link in your receipt email.
  2. Change your answers, the preview or your drawings.
  3. Choose Redo. There’s no charge.

If something goes wrong on our side

If a paid build fails or never arrives, email us with your receipt and we will run it again at no cost.

Cancellation

Studio FGA has no subscriptions. Each board and deck is a one-time payment, nothing renews, and you are never charged again unless you pay for another project. There is nothing to cancel.

Payments

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. If you were charged twice or see a charge you don’t recognise, email us first with the order number from your receipt and we’ll sort it out with Dodo.

Your rights

This policy doesn’t affect any rights you have under consumer law that cannot be excluded.
