    FAQ

    Questions

    How do I lay out an architecture presentation board?

    Decide the two to four ideas the board must explain, then let one strong image lead and give every other drawing a job: a plan, a section or a diagram that proves one idea. Keep a clear reading order, plans and sections at stated scales, short captions and plenty of white space. FGA Studio plans this story with you and lays it out on a grid you can still change.

    What size is an architecture presentation board?

    Usually A1 (594 × 841 mm) in the UK, Europe, India and Australia, and 24 × 36 in (ARCH D) in the US; some schools ask for A0 (841 × 1189 mm) at final reviews. Check your studio brief. FGA Studio makes all three and a 16:9 slide deck.

    Which formats can I make?

    An A1, A0 or 24 × 36 in board, or a slide deck. Square posts and a web deck come later. You can switch before you export.

    How much does it cost?

    You buy a one-time pack of prepaid credits and use them to export your boards and decks; there is no subscription and nothing renews. [[FILL: One credit exports one board or one deck.]] You see a free, watermarked preview before you pay anything. Packs and prices are on the pricing page.

    Does it work with PowerPoint and Canva?

    Yes. You get an editable PowerPoint file: a whole A1 board sits on one slide, and it opens in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides and Canva. You also get a print-ready PDF.

    Is it allowed for my coursework?

    It lays out your drawings and draws the ideas you point to; the design and the thinking are yours. Words are written only from your own brief. Check your school’s rules on AI tools, and say you used it if they ask.

    Do my drawings stay private?

    Yes. Your files are made into the board in your own browser, not on our servers: they never leave your device. See the Privacy Policy.
