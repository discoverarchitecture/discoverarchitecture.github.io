Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 (added FGA Studio accounts, uploads and payments)

Find Great Architecture (findgreatarchitecture.com) is an independent project run by Akshat Pagaria, an individual based in India (“I”, “me”). This policy covers the free website, FGA Studio (the paid design tool at findgreatarchitecture.com/studio/) and the official Find Great Architecture accounts on Instagram and YouTube.

The short version

  • The wall of buildings and the games have no accounts and no payments.
  • FGA Studio has accounts and payments. There I keep your account email and a record of your credits and purchases. Your drawings and photos are made into the board in your own browser and never sent to me. Payments are handled by Dodo Payments; I never see your card details.
  • The FGA Studio waitlist keeps your email address only if you choose to join it.
  • The website shows no ads, and the wall and games set no cookies.
  • Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and does not follow you across other websites.
  • I never sell or rent information about anyone.

When you visit the website

Visitor counting. GoatCounter records which pages are opened, the page you arrived from (including tags such as ?ref=instagram), your browser, device type and screen size, and your approximate country, worked out from your IP address. It does not store your IP address and sets no cookies. I use the totals to see which buildings people find useful. GoatCounter’s privacy policy: https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy

Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (FGA Studio itself is hosted on Cloudflare; see below). Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address in order to deliver the pages and may keep it in its logs for security. GitHub’s Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Fonts. The website’s typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers and Google receives your IP address. Google says it uses this only to deliver the fonts: https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy

Your own browser. The building quiz and a few display preferences (such as your daily progress and streak) are saved in your browser’s local storage. They stay on your device, are never sent to me, and are deleted when you clear this site’s data.

Links. Building pages link to plans, articles, archives and bookshops on other websites. Once you follow a link, that website’s own privacy policy applies.

The FGA Studio waitlist

FGA Studio (findgreatarchitecture.com/studio/) is a tool I am building. If you join its waitlist, I keep your email address, whether you are a student, a teacher or an architect, and, if you answer them, what you are presenting next and when your next jury is. I also keep where you came from (a tag such as ?ref=ig) and the page you signed up on.

  • I use this only to email you when FGA Studio opens and about your early-access discount, and to understand what students need. Nothing else, and never for advertising.
  • It is stored in a private Google Sheet in my own Google account, sent there through Google Apps Script. Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • I never share, sell or rent it. Each sign-up is also counted in GoatCounter as a number, without your email address.
  • To leave the waitlist or have your details deleted, email me (address below) or reply to any waitlist email. I delete them within 30 days and confirm by email.

FGA Studio: accounts, files and payments

FGA Studio turns your own drawings, renders and photos into an editable jury board and slide deck. It is still being built and opens in November 2026. Until then nobody can make an account or buy credits, so none of the details below have been collected yet. Before it opens, I will add the details that depend on the finished app (whether signing in uses a cookie, and the name of any outside AI service that helps plan the board and exactly what it receives) and change the date at the top of this page.

Your files stay on your device. FGA Studio makes your board and deck in your own browser. Your drawings, renders, photos and brief are not sent to me, and I never see, store, publish or train anything on them.

What I keep.

  • Your account: your email address.
  • Your credits: how many credits you bought and when you used them.
  • Payment-related details from Dodo Payments: the order number, the pack you bought, the amount and currency, your country, the date, whether the payment went through, and the email address on the order. Dodo Payments is the merchant of record for FGA Studio purchases and handles the payment itself. I never see or store your card number or bank details.

Why. To run your account, keep your credit balance right, answer support and refund requests, and keep the records that tax and accounting law requires. Nothing else, and never for advertising.

Who else handles it. Dodo Payments, for payments and receipts: Dodo Payments’ Privacy Policy. Cloudflare hosts FGA Studio, so your browser connects to Cloudflare’s servers and Cloudflare receives your IP address in order to deliver the app: Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

How long I keep it.

  • Your account: kept while you use it. Ask me to close it and I delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Purchase records: kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after your account is closed.

Instagram and YouTube

I publish the project’s own posts to @findgreatarchitecture on Instagram and YouTube using Meta’s Instagram API and the YouTube API Services. These connections act only on my own accounts. They publish my posts, read statistics about my own posts (such as views, reach, likes, saves and shares), and read comments and messages that people send to my accounts, so that I can reply.

  • I do not collect data from anyone’s personal account, and I do not use any of it for advertising or profiling.
  • Comments and messages sent to my accounts are stored only in encrypted form, used only to read and answer them, and never shared or sold. Post statistics are kept for up to 120 days.
  • This project uses YouTube API Services. When you watch or interact with my videos on YouTube, the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) apply.
  • The only Google account connected to these tools is the channel owner’s. Its access can be revoked at any time on Google’s security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
  • Instagram’s own handling of your data is covered by Meta’s Privacy Policy: https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy

Data deletion

If you would like a comment or message you sent to my Instagram or YouTube accounts removed from my records, email me with your username and where you posted it. I will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting it on Instagram or YouTube yourself also removes it from my records at the next update. Waitlist details are deleted the same way (see above), and FGA Studio accounts as set out in the FGA Studio section. Apart from those, the website holds no personal data about you to delete.

Children

This project is made for architecture students and anyone interested in buildings. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR and California’s privacy laws, you can ask what information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it or to stop using it. In practice this applies to your FGA Studio account and purchase records, your waitlist details, and comments or messages you have sent to my social accounts. Purchase records that tax law requires me to keep cannot be deleted early. Email me and I will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where data is processed

The services this project relies on (GitHub, GoatCounter, Google, Meta, Dodo Payments, Cloudflare) may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and material changes are noted on this page.
