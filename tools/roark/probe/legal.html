Content and rights.

The archive

Photographs, plans and drawings in the archive come from open collections: Wikimedia Commons, Europeana, the Art Institute of Chicago, and OpenStreetMap for site plans. Each image is shown with its author, its licence and a link to its source, as those licences require.

What members bring

Members add images of their own: uploaded from their computer, saved from a link, or imported from Cosmos or Pinterest. Each keeps its author and its source when they are known. Rights stay with the author.

Once reviewed, a member's image appears in their collections and on their profile. Images of architecture also appear in the main feed, and on the page of the building they show when Roark recognises it or the member names it. Images that are not architecture stay in the member's own collections.

Architects and photographers can mark images as their own work. Those are shown on the building's page, credited to them, with a link to their profile. Adding your folders explains how.

The licence members give Roark on their own work, and how they can remove it, are in the terms. By adding an image, a member confirms they are allowed to share it. Accounts that repeatedly add images they have no right to share are closed.

Review

Every image a member adds is checked before anyone else can see it. A vision model reads it for two things: whether it shows architecture, and whether it resembles sexual, violent or hateful imagery. Images that resemble the latter stay hidden until a person has looked at them. Large batches are held for up to two days so a person can review them first.

Not allowed, anywhere on Roark: sexual content, any sexualised depiction of a minor, graphic violence, hate symbols and propaganda, harassment, and images of private people shared without their consent. Such images are removed and the account is closed. Images that may involve a crime against a child are reported to the authorities.

Each account, and each network, can add a limited number of images per day. The limits are generous for real work and leave no room for floods.

Taking an image down

Members remove their own images from the image's page; the file is deleted with it.

If you hold the rights to an image on Roark and want it removed, use Report on its page, or write to [email protected] with the link to the image, the work you hold the rights to, and how to reach you. The same address takes reports of anything that breaks these rules. Copyright and safety reports are reviewed first; images are taken down when the claim holds.
