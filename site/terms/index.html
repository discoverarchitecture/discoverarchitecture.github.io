What FGA Studio is

FGA Studio is still being built and opens in November 2026. Until then there is a waitlist and nothing can be bought; this part of the terms applies from the day it opens. FGA Studio is a web design tool for architecture students. You upload your own drawings, renders, photos and a short brief, and it makes an editable jury presentation board and slide deck (PowerPoint and PDF files). It uses AI to plan the board’s layout, draw the diagrams you point to and prepare your photos for print. It runs in your web browser and is hosted on Cloudflare. It is a digital service: nothing physical is sent.
