UNStudio

Founded 1988 · Dutch · 3 buildings on the wall

UNStudio is a Dutch architecture practice in Amsterdam, founded in 1988 by Ben van Berkel and Caroline Bos as Van Berkel en Bos Architectenbureau. The initials stand for United Network, a reference to the collaborative, international team it draws on; it now has more than 250 staff and offices in Shanghai, Hong Kong, Frankfurt, Dubai and Melbourne. Its work runs from infrastructure to houses: early projects included the Erasmus Bridge in Rotterdam (1996) and the Möbius House (1998), and later ones Arnhem Centraal station (2015) and the stations of the Doha Metro.

The Galleria Department Store (West) in Seoul (2004) was given a new façade of 4,330 glass discs on its concrete skin, with a foil that gives a mother-of-pearl sheen by day and LED lights behind each disc that can be programmed at night. The Mercedes-Benz Museum in Stuttgart (2006) is built on a cloverleaf of three overlapping circles around a triangular atrium; a double-helix route fits 16,500 square metres of exhibition space onto a 4,800-square-metre footprint and lets visitors switch between two tours. The Agora Theatre in Lelystad (2007) followed.
