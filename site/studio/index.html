How Studio FGA works

  1. Upload your drawings: plans, sections, elevations, sketches, renders and model photos (PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF).
  2. Answer a few quick questions: what you are making (a jury board, design review, site analysis, precedent study, portfolio page or client presentation), your name, project title, location and program, how far AI should go, and your concept in a few words.
  3. Pay once: Polish $6.49 or Transform $9.34. Your board and deck are ready in about 5 to 7 minutes.
  4. Edit in Studio's editor and download. Not jury-ready? One free redo, or your money back within 7 days.

What you get

One A1 architecture presentation board and a matching 10–15 slide jury deck, editable, as PDF, PowerPoint and InDesign files. Keep editing in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, Canva or InDesign.

Pricing

Polish, US$6.49: one A1 board and one jury deck, up to 10 new images. Transform, US$9.34: one A1 board and one jury deck, up to 15 new images. One payment each, no subscription. Not jury-ready? One free redo, or your money back within 7 days. See pricing.

FAQ

What if I don't like the result?

Not jury-ready? One free redo, or your money back within 7 days. Email hello@findgreatarchitecture.com with your order number.

Can I keep editing after I download?

Yes. Swap pictures, crop them and fix any line in Studio's editor, then keep going in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, Canva or InDesign.

How long does it take?

About 5 to 7 minutes after you pay. We email you the link to your board and deck when they are ready.

What happens to my files?

Your uploads and your finished board and deck are kept for 30 days so you can reopen, edit and redo them, then deleted. We don't sell your data or use your files to train AI models.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. A laptop is easier for editing a big board, and it is usually where your drawings are.
