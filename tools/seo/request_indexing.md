# Ask Google to look again: building pages that now have a write-up

Google had read these pages before 2 Nov 2026 and left them out of its index ("Crawled – currently not indexed" in
`search_console/indexing.json` on the `analytics-data` branch). Each now has its own fact-checked paragraph, so it is
worth asking Google to read it again. Search Console allows about ten requests a day.

How (owner, in Search Console): paste the address into the search bar at the top, wait for the result, then press
**Request indexing**. Ten a day, from the top. The sitemap now dates every page with a write-up (`<lastmod>`), so Google
will reach the rest by itself; these are the ones Google showed most often, so they go first.

368 write-up pages were not indexed on 2 Nov; the 50 most-shown are below (impressions: the 28 days in `latest.json`).
