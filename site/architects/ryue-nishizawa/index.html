Architect

Ryue Nishizawa

Born 1966 · Japanese · 7 buildings on the wall

Ryue Nishizawa (born 1966) is a Japanese architect based in Tokyo. A graduate of Yokohama National University, he co-founded SANAA with Kazuyo Sejima in 1995 and set up his own practice, the Office of Ryue Nishizawa, in 1997. In 2010 he and Sejima received the Pritzker Prize, and at 44 he became its youngest winner.

Read moreShow less

His own projects include the Moriyama House in Tokyo (2005). The Towada Art Center (2008) opened as part of a project to revive the city of Towada, and the Teshima Art Museum (2010), on an island in Japan's Seto Inland Sea, is a single concrete shell 25 centimetres thick, 40 by 60 metres and four metres high at most, holding one work by the artist Rei Naito. He later completed the Hiroshi Senju Museum Karuizawa (2011) and the Garden & House in Tokyo (2011). With Sejima, as SANAA, he designed the New Museum in New York (2007), a stack of white boxes on the Bowery. Ryue Nishizawa's work is studied for thin structure and spaces that blur inside and out.
