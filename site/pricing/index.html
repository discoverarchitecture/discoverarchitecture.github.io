Pricing

Studio FGA · last updated: 29 October 2026

Studio FGA turns an architecture student’s own plans, sections, sketches and model photos into a jury-ready A1 presentation board and a matching 10–15 slide jury deck, using AI. You see a free preview of your whole board and deck before you pay. Everything is digital; nothing physical is sent.

Two options, one payment each

  • One payment per project. There is no subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Checkout shows them in your local currency, with any sales tax or VAT, before you pay.
  • Both options include the editor, files as PDF, PowerPoint and InDesign, and one free redo within 7 days.

Payment

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record for Studio FGA: it takes the payment, handles any sales tax or VAT and sends your receipt. Studio FGA never sees your card or bank details.

Refunds

All sales are final and there are no refunds, because each board and deck is made for you as soon as you pay. If the result isn’t right, you get one free redo within 7 days. If a paid build fails on our side, we run it again at no cost. The details are in the Refund Policy.

Your files

Your uploads and your finished board and deck are kept for 30 days so you can reopen, edit and redo them, then deleted. See the Studio FGA Privacy Policy.

Email hello@findgreatarchitecture.com. See the contact page for what to include.
