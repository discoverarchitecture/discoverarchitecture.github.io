# Further reading: architects_01 (48 architects)

Done 21 Oct 2026. Candidates came from web search (cloud chats can only open Wikipedia); every kept link was opened by
`tools/check_reading_links.py` on GitHub Actions. Pages are trimmed to at most 15 links, at most 3 per site, paid at
most a third, and the one non-English page found (Domus Italian page for SOM) and a Danish encyclopaedia entry were dropped.

- 46 of 48 pages have 10 to 15 links.
- **Richard Meier: 9 links.** The only other strong sources found are about single buildings, or are about the 2018
  allegations rather than the work; nothing padded.
- **Kunio Maekawa: 7 links.** English-language writing on him is thin; the rest of the search hits were single
  buildings or Japanese pages.
- Links that answered 403/404 to the checker (many MoMA, Smarthistory, Met 429s) were left out; they may load for a
  reader, so a later pass could restore some by hand.
- The NYT, Guardian, New Yorker, BBC and FT could not be searched from the cloud chat, so no paid links from them;
  the list is mostly free sources. A pass with those publishers would add the paid links the brief allows.
