Architect

Christoph Ingenhoven

Born 1960 · German · 3 buildings on the wall

Christoph Ingenhoven (born 1960) is a German architect based in Düsseldorf, known for buildings designed around daylight, natural ventilation and low energy use, an approach he calls "supergreen". Born in Düsseldorf, he studied at RWTH Aachen and, for a year, with Hans Hollein at the Kunstakademie Düsseldorf, and founded his office in the city in 1985. He drew wide notice in 1991, when his team shared the jury prize with Norman Foster in the competition for the Commerzbank Tower in Frankfurt, and international recognition with the RWE Tower in Essen (1997), one of the world's first ecological high-rise buildings. He received the European Prize for Architecture in 2022.

Read moreShow less

His completed works include the European Investment Bank in Luxembourg (2008). 1 Bligh Street in Sydney (2011), designed with Architectus, is a 30-storey office tower with Australia's first full double-skin façade on a major high-rise, external louvres that turn with the sun and a naturally ventilated atrium 120 metres high; it won the International High-Rise Award. The Lanserhof Sylt (2022) won both MIPIM and WAF awards in 2023.
