Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Draft for owner review

This policy explains what Studio FGA (findgreatarchitecture.com/studio) collects and why. Studio is run by Akshat Pagaria, 302, Shantinath Residency, Beawar, Rajasthan, India. Contact: hello@findgreatarchitecture.com.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Files you upload (drawings, sketches, photos)To make your board and deck
Your answers to the questions, including the name, studio, tutor and year you typeTo write and lay out your board and deck
Your email addressTo send your receipt and a link back to your files
Payment confirmation from Dodo Payments (amount, product, date)To unlock your build. We never see your card or bank details
Your rating and any feedback noteTo improve Studio
Basic technical data (browser, errors)To keep Studio working

We don't sell your data, and we don't use your files to train AI models.

While Studio is new, Akshat (who runs Studio) personally looks at uploads and finished boards and decks to check quality and fix problems. Nobody else sees them.

Who processes it for us

We use these services only to run Studio:

  • Dodo Payments: payment and tax (merchant of record)
  • Anthropic (Claude): reading your drawings and writing text
  • fal.ai: re-rendering and redrawing images
  • Modal: running the build
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the website
  • An email service to send your receipt

Some of these process data outside India, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

Your uploaded files and finished board and deck are kept for 30 days so you can reopen, edit and redo them, then deleted. Receipts are kept as long as tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your files sooner.

Your choices

  • Email hello@findgreatarchitecture.com to see, correct or delete your data.
  • We feature your board publicly only if you say yes, and you can withdraw that at any time.

Children

Studio is for people aged 16 and over, or younger with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
