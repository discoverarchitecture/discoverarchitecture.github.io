Nieto Sobejano Arquitectos

Founded 1984 · Spanish · 4 buildings on the wall

Nieto Sobejano Arquitectos is an architecture practice founded in 1984 by Fuensanta Nieto and Enrique Sobejano, both born in Madrid in 1957, with offices in Madrid and, since 2007, Berlin. The two studied at the Polytechnic University of Madrid and at Columbia University in New York, and both teach. Their work has been shown at the Venice Biennale and the Museum of Modern Art, and the practice has received the Aga Khan Award for Architecture (2010), the European Museum of the Year Award (2012) and the Alvar Aalto Medal (2015). Much of their work adds to historic buildings and sites.

Read moreShow less

Their major works include the Kunstmuseum Moritzburg extension in Halle, Germany (2008), the Madinat al-Zahra Museum at Córdoba (2009), one of two projects in that city, and the San Telmo Museum extension in San Sebastián (2011); in 2007 the practice received Spain's National Prize for Restoration. The Arvo Pärt Centre at Laulasmaa in Estonia (2018), won in competition in 2014, stays below the canopy of a pine forest except for an observation tower, its glass walls joining an oak-panelled interior to the trees.
